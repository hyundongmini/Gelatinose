The premise
2030. Earth fashion becomes a galactic hit — and its biggest buyers are not from Earth.
Off-world species have developed a taste for terrestrial dress. At the center of the trend are the Gelatinos: a translucent, gel-bodied species whose proportions no Earth pattern was ever cut for.
Our client is Neo-Cosmos, the distributor serving that market. The project is a cross-species high-fashion collection built for the Gelatinos — posters, product, and print made for a body that will not hold its shape.
